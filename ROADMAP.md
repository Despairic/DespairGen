# DespairGen Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the major systems and improvements planned for future DespairGen development.

DespairGen v0.6.1 is currently the public holdover release. Development for the next major release builds upon this version and continues the experimental work originally explored during v0.7 development.

---

# Next Major Release

## Player & Clan Movement

- [ ] Expand player-character movement between groups and Clans.
- [ ] Add and refine Leave Group functionality.
- [ ] Support different paths such as joining another Clan, becoming a loner, or becoming a rogue.
- [ ] Improve handling of the player's active group.
- [ ] Ensure relationships, mentorships, roles, and events properly update after group changes.
- [ ] Populate destination Clans when necessary without unnecessarily generating every Clan.

## Faith & Afterlife

- [ ] Expand StarClan and Dark Forest gameplay.
- [ ] Further integrate faith into gameplay and consequences.
- [ ] Expand interactions with afterlife cats.
- [ ] Properly fix the Dark Forest Follow interaction currently disabled in v0.6.1.
- [ ] Continue expanding rare and hidden encounters.

## Leadership & Shunning

- [ ] Further refine leader shunning and succession.
- [ ] Improve deputy selection and succession behavior.
- [ ] Continue integrating faith into leadership decisions.
- [ ] Ensure leadership changes work correctly when the player changes groups.

## Relationships

- [ ] Continue expanding relationship and dating systems.
- [ ] Further improve larger relationship-group support.
- [ ] Refine relationship restrictions and interactions.
- [ ] Improve relationship handling when cats change groups or Clans.

## Achievements & Secrets

- [ ] Expand the DespairGen achievement system.
- [ ] Add additional secret achievements.
- [ ] Add more rare encounters and hidden content.
- [ ] Continue expanding special achievement-related content.

> Achievement requirements, names, exact conditions, probabilities, and other secrets will remain undisclosed until discovered in-game.

## UI & Quality of Life

- [ ] Add a DespairGen tutorial.
- [ ] Continue improving cat profiles.
- [ ] Continue refining colored role names.
- [ ] Improve major game screens and interfaces.
- [ ] Add additional quality-of-life improvements.

## Stability

- [ ] Replace temporary v0.6.1 workarounds with proper fixes.
- [ ] Continue fixing crashes and stale-reference issues.
- [ ] Improve stability around Clan/group changes.
- [ ] Continue regression testing before major releases.

---

- [ ] Additional DespairGen-exclusive gameplay systems.
- [ ] Further afterlife mechanics.
- [ ] Additional customization options.
- [ ] More relationship and social mechanics.
- [ ] More rare encounters and events.
- [ ] Additional hidden/easter-egg content.

# Deferred / Not Currently Planned

Nothing currently listed.

---

# Roadmap Notes

- This roadmap is subject to change as development continues.
- Features are not considered officially planned until they are agreed upon during development.
- Experimental features may change significantly before release.
- Stability and proper system-level fixes take priority over rushing new features into a release.
- Secret content will not be publicly documented before players have a chance to discover it.
